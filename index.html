Asymmetric Beta Analysis

Indian Sectoral Indices using an ARIMAX Framework

Empirical Analysis of Non-Linear Market Sensitivity

Project Overview

This project examines whether Indian sectoral indices respond differently to positive and negative movements in the overall market. The analysis uses the Nifty 50 as the market benchmark and applies an ARIMAX framework to estimate asymmetric beta relationships.

Methodology

The study uses weekly market data and estimates sectoral sensitivity separately during positive and negative market movements.


  • Log return calculation
  • Positive and negative market regime decomposition
  • ARIMAX model estimation
  • AIC-based ARIMAX order selection
  • Wald test for asymmetric beta
  • Stationarity diagnostics
  • Serial correlation diagnostics
  • ARCH-LM heteroscedasticity test
  • Jarque-Bera normality test

Research Focus

The analysis investigates whether sectoral market sensitivity differs between positive and negative market conditions. The key comparison is between β⁺, representing sensitivity during positive market movements, and β⁻, representing sensitivity during negative market movements.
