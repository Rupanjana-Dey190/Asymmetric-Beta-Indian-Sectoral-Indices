Asymmetric Beta Analysis

Indian Sectoral Indices using an ARIMAX Framework
Empirical Analysis of Non-Linear Market Sensitivity

Project Overview

This project examines whether Indian sectoral indices respond differently to positive and negative movements in the overall market. The Nifty 50 is used as the market benchmark, and an ARIMAX framework is applied to estimate asymmetric beta relationships.

Methodology

Weekly sectoral and market data are transformed into log returns. Market returns are then separated into positive and negative regimes to estimate whether sectoral market sensitivity changes across market conditions.

D⁺ₜ = max(rₘₖₜ, 0)      D⁻ₜ = min(rₘₖₜ, 0)
  • Log return calculation
  • Positive and negative market regime decomposition
  • ARIMAX(p,0,q) modelling
  • AIC-based grid search for p and q from 0–3
  • Wald test for H₀: β⁺ = β⁻
  • ADF and KPSS stationarity tests
  • Ljung-Box serial correlation test
  • ARCH-LM heteroscedasticity test
  • Jarque-Bera normality test

Research Focus

The analysis focuses on whether sectoral market sensitivity differs between positive and negative market movements. The key parameters are β⁺, representing sensitivity during positive market movements, and β⁻, representing sensitivity during negative market movements.
